# Evidence E04 — PIM resource-role review

**Evidence type:** Learner observation summary. The original Azure Portal screenshots are retained privately, not republished.

During the authorized training exercise, I observed:

1. An **Eligible** custom Azure-resource role associated with a resource-group scope.
2. An activation request that required a justification and presented an activation-duration control.
3. An **Active**, time-bound state after activation.
4. A separate PIM **Groups** membership option, distinct from Azure-resource role activation.

**Interpretation:** Azure-resource PIM controls when an eligible scoped RBAC role may be activated; PIM for Groups controls membership. The permissions available after activation depend on the actual role definitions, group assignments, and scopes.

**What these observations do not establish:** Every tenant-wide assignment, a complete PIM activation or approval history, or a verified Owner grant to the operative account. Other safeguards vary by role policy.

Public screenshots were withheld because the originals show private resource and account identifiers.
